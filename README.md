## 🔥 What Makes PackCheck AI Epic?

* **⚡ Gemini Multimodal Vision:** Instantly scans front/back food product labels to decode ingredients and nutritional values.
* **🛑 Health Risk Matrix:** Flags high-sodium, high-sugar, harmful preservatives, and MSG alerts in real-time.
* **🥗 Smart Alternative Swaps:** Recommends healthier organic substitutes with calorie comparison metrics.
* **🛡️ FSSAI Grievance Docket System:** Generates official consumer grievance reports for misleading product claims.
* **📜 Secure Scan History:** Local storage tracking for all previously audited packages with quick-access cards.
